# Privacy Policy for Velox Speedometer

**Last updated:** August 26, 2026

**Velox Speedometer** (\com.titanforge.velox\), developed by **Titanforge**, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user data.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Tracking

Velox Speedometer does **NOT** collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal information, location history, or usage data to external servers or third parties.

- **No User Accounts**: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details to use the app.
- **No Third-Party Analytics / Trackers**: We do not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in this version.

---

## 2. Location Permission (\ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION\ / \ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION\)

Velox Speedometer requires access to your device's GPS hardware solely to provide real-time functionality:
- Calculating current speed (km/h, mph, knots)
- Measuring trip distance
- Estimating altitude and compass heading

**Important**: All GPS calculations are performed strictly on-device in real-time. Your location coordinates are never saved to permanent storage, nor are they transmitted over the internet.

---

## 3. Local Settings Storage

The app stores user preferences (such as selected language, speed unit, speed limit threshold, and gauge theme) locally on your device using Android's standard \SharedPreferences\. This data never leaves your device.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Velox Speedometer does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.
