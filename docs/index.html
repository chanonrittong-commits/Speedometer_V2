Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

🔒 100% Privacy & Zero Data Collection

Velox Speedometer is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. We do not collect, track, store, transmit, or share any personal information, driving telemetry, or location data with external servers or third parties.

🛰️ 1. Real-Time Location Access (GPS)

Velox Speedometer requests runtime access to your device's precise location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) solely to calculate and display:

  • Current Speed: Converted into your chosen unit (km/h, mph, knots).
  • Trip Distance & Max Speed: Accumulated telemetry during your current session.
  • Altitude & Heading: Real-time elevation and cardinal compass orientation.

On-Device Processing Only: All GPS calculations are processed in volatile memory on your device in real time. Coordinates are never written to permanent logs, nor are they transmitted across any network.

🚫 2. Zero Third-Party Trackers & Ads

This application contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and no user profiling tools. We do not sell or monetize user data.

💾 3. Local Device Preferences

Preferences such as your selected display language (English / Thai), speed units (KM/H, MPH, KTS), speed limit warning thresholds, and gauge theme choices are stored locally on your device using Android's standard SharedPreferences. This preference data never leaves your device.

👶 4. Children's Privacy

Velox Speedometer does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for all audiences.

🔄 5. Changes to This Policy

If we make any updates to our Privacy Policy in future versions, the updated version will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
